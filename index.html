How to install on your phone (Android)
  1. Do this on your Android phone, not a computer.
  2. Tap Download the App above to get the app file.
  3. If Chrome says the file can harm your device, tap the download arrow ▾ on the file, then choose Keep.
  4. Open app-release.apk from the download notification or your Files app.
  5. If Play Protect shows "Unknown app", tap More details → Install anyway.
  6. If asked to allow installs from this source, tap Allow, then Install.
  7. Tap Open — you're done.
